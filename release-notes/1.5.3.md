# SCrip 1.5.3 for Android

Universal APK for Android 9+, supporting ARM64, ARMv7 and x86-64. Desktop installers were not updated in this release.

- Prepare the next track without silent playback; improved background buffering and temporary audio-focus loss handling.
- Save the queue, current track and position to resume listening.
- Added a loading indicator, player swipes, back navigation, artist-profile navigation and likes on reposts.
- Removed extra explanations from the plugin page and clarified the message when no update is available for the platform.

Actual stream stability, battery use and resuming after other apps depend on the device and network. Android may not notify the app when permanent audio focus returns.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`; sources and licensing information accompany the release. The APK is signed with the key used for previous releases.

[Inventory manifest and sources](https://github.com/sykia/SCrip/tree/v1.5.3/licensing/releases/1.5.3). Unchanged dependency sources are available in the [1.4.0 materials](https://github.com/sykia/SCrip/releases/download/v1.4.0/compliance.tar.gz).
